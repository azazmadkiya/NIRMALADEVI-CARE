⚠️ Chemical Safety & Disclaimer

DANGER — HAZARDOUS INDUSTRIAL CHEMICALS Sulphuric Acid (98%), Hydrochloric Acid (33%), Nitric Acid (68%), Hydrofluoric Acid (40-60%), and Caustic Soda Lye/Flakes are highly corrosive and toxic industrial raw materials.

1. Mandatory Industrial PPE Protocols

Before handling, unloading, sampling, or mixing any chemicals supplied by Nirmaladevi Care Pvt. Ltd., plant personnel must be equipped with certified Personal Protective Equipment:

🛡️ Face & Eye Protection

Full-face chemical splash shield and chemical-resistant safety goggles at all times.

🧤 Heavy-Duty Gloves

Neoprene, Nitrile, or Butyl rubber gloves (minimum 14–18 inch gauntlet length).

🥾 Protective Apparel

Acid/alkali-resistant PVC apron, chemical safety coveralls, and steel-toe rubber gumboots.

🚿 Emergency Facilities

Functioning emergency eye-wash station and deluge safety shower within 10 seconds reach.

2. Chemical Dilution Rule

NEVER ADD WATER TO CONCENTRATED ACID (Always Add Acid to Water slowly with continuous stirring). Adding water to concentrated Sulphuric Acid causes instant exothermic boiling, splattering, and severe thermal and chemical burns.

3. In-App Calculator Disclaimer

The mathematical utilities inside the Nirmaladevi Care application (such as Dilution $C_1 V_1 = C_2 V_2$ and Mass-Volume Specific Gravity conversions) are provided solely as theoretical estimations. Laboratory chemists and plant supervisors are strictly required to perform physical titration and specific gravity hydrometer checks prior to production-scale formulation.
