🛡️ Privacy Policy
Effective Date: August 24, 2025
Application: Nirmaladevi Care (Android)
Package: com.aistudio.nirmaladevicare.cptrade

Nirmaladevi Care Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Nirmaladevi Care mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding how we handle information, maintain data safety, and protect privacy in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable data protection laws.

1. Information We Collect

We do NOT track your GPS in the background or inspect private files. We only process information voluntarily provided by you:

  • Quotation Inquiries: Company/Factory name, delivery location, contact phone number, and chemical requirement notes.
  • Direct Communications: Message contents transmitted when you contact our Morbi sales desk via WhatsApp, Phone, or Email.
  • Local Calculations: All chemical dilution and specific gravity calculations run 100% on your local device.

2. How We Use Information

  • Generating commercial chemical quotations, invoices, and dispatch schedules.
  • Providing technical guidance, purity specs (CP, Tech, Food Grade), and Certificates of Analysis (COA).
  • Facilitating road tanker, IBC tank, and carboy chemical deliveries to your designated plant.

3. No Data Selling / Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or commercial inquiry data to third parties or advertising networks.

⚠️ Chemical Safety & Disclaimer
MANDATORY SAFETY NOTICE:
Chemicals listed in our catalogue (such as Sulphuric Acid, Hydrochloric Acid, Hydrofluoric Acid, Nitric Acid, and Caustic Soda) are hazardous industrial substances.

1. Industrial Safety Standards

All chemical products must only be handled by certified factory personnel wearing full Personal Protective Equipment (PPE: acid-resistant gloves, face shield, rubber apron, safety boots) in accordance with government safety norms and official Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS).

2. Dilution Calculator Disclaimer

The Dilution and Mass-Volume calculation tools provided inside this app are mathematical estimation utilities provided in good faith. Plant supervisors must verify exact laboratory titration values and specific gravity before carrying out large-scale batch mixing.

🗑️ Account & Data Deletion
User Data Safety & Deletion Policy
How to clear your local application state or request deletion of quotation inquiries.

1. On-Device Data Clearing

The Nirmaladevi Care app stores your drafts and calculator state locally on your device. You can immediately wipe all data at any time:

  • Open your device Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Nirmaladevi Care.
  • Tap on Storage & Cache ➔ Clear Storage / Clear Data.

2. Requesting Inquiry Deletion

If you have previously submitted an inquiry via email or WhatsApp and wish to have your contact details purged from our customer registry, email us with the subject "Data Deletion Request":

📧 Email: nirmaladevicarepvtltd@gmail.com

All requests are processed within 48 to 72 business hours.
