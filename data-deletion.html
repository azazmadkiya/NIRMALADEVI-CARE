🗑️ Account & Data Deletion

Application: Nirmaladevi Care (Android)
Package Name: com.aistudio.nirmaladevicare.cptrade
Compliance: Google Play User Data Policy & Account Deletion Requirement

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and data protection standards, this page outlines clear instructions for wiping local device data or requesting permanent deletion of quotation inquiries.

Method 1: Instant Local Device Data Wiping

📱 Clear Storage on your Android Smartphone

The Nirmaladevi Care application operates on a local-first architecture. All drafted quotes, search histories, and calculator parameters are stored directly on your phone. You can wipe everything instantly:

  1. Open your Android phone's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps or App Management.
  3. Select Nirmaladevi Care from the app list.
  4. Tap on Storage & Cache.
  5. Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data) and confirm.

Result: All local application preferences, drafts, and cached specifications are permanently erased from your device.

Method 2: Request Commercial Inquiry Records Deletion

✉️ Email-Based Inquiry Records Purging

If you have previously submitted an inquiry via WhatsApp, email, or telephone and wish to delete your business contact records from our sales desk:

  • Send an email to: nirmaladevicarepvtltd@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Data Deletion Request - [Your Company Name / Phone]
  • Include: The phone number or email address associated with your past quotation inquiries.

Timeline: Our administrative compliance team processes deletion requests within 48 to 72 business hours and sends a written confirmation email.

3. Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Immediately: Factory name, contact phone numbers, drafted quotation logs, custom packaging notes.
  • Legally Mandated Retention: Official GST tax invoices already issued for completed sales transactions are retained for statutory accounting compliance periods as mandated by Indian tax law.
