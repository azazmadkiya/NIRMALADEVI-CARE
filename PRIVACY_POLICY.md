## 1. 🏢 Nature & Purpose of the Application
The **Nirmaladevi Care** app is a specialized B2B industrial chemical trading catalogue, quotation inquiry generator, and chemist utility toolkit created for industrial manufacturing plants, ceramic factories, chemical dealers, and laboratory chemists.

Key features include:
- Browsing industrial chemical specifications, purity grades, and packaging configurations.
- Preparing commercial purchase inquiries and dispatch requests.
- Local solution dilution ($C_1 V_1 = C_2 V_2$) and Mass-to-Volume estimations.
- Saving draft inquiries locally using an on-device SQLite database via Android Room.

---

## 2. 📊 Information Collection and Processing
We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We do **not** collect background telemetry, device sensors, location tracking, or private files.

### A. Voluntary User-Entered Commercial Information
When you prepare an inquiry or request quotation pricing, you may voluntarily enter:
- **Contact Person / User Name**
- **Company / Factory Name**
- **Delivery Destination / Plant Location**
- **Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number**
- **Requested Chemical Items, Quantities & Packaging Preferences**

### B. On-Device Chemical Calculations
All dilution calculations and mass/volume unit conversions are computed 100% locally on your device. No calculation variables or formulas are uploaded to external servers.

### C. Offline Local Storage (Room Database)
Pending inquiry drafts and user preference entries are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database using Android Jetpack Room. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly initiate an inquiry dispatch.

---

## 3. 🎯 How We Use Collected Information
Data entered into the application is used strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:
- To generate price estimates, proforma inquiries, and dispatch schedules for industrial chemical orders.
- To facilitate direct business communication with our sales and technical support desk in Morbi, Gujarat.
- To provide Certificates of Analysis (COA) and Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) upon request.

## 4. 🚫 Data Sharing, Selling & Third-Party Disclosure
- **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** The application contains **no advertisements**, no tracking SDKs, and no behavioral analytics cookies.

  - **Email Client:** Drafted directly in your default email client addressed to `nirmaladevicarepvtltd@gmail.com`.

## 5. 🔐 Data Security & Storage
- We employ strict Android security best practices.
- Inquiry records and preferences saved locally in Room SQLite database remain sandboxed in private application storage.
- You can clear all cached records and local databases anytime directly from Android Settings (`Settings > Apps > Nirmaladevi Care > Storage > Clear Data`).

## 6. 🗑️ Data Retention & Deletion Policy
Users have the full right to delete their information:
1. **Local App Data:** Clear storage via Android system settings or using the in-app *Clear All* button on the Inquiry/Cart screens.
2. **Communication Records:** If you have submitted inquiries via WhatsApp or Email and wish to remove your contact details from our sales records, send a deletion request to [nirmaladevicarepvtltd@gmail.com](mailto:nirmaladevicarepvtltd@gmail.com) with the subject *"Data Deletion Request"*.
