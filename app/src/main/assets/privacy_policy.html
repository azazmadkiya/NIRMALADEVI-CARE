Privacy Policy for Nirmaladevi Care

Last Updated: August 23, 2025 | Effective Date: August 23, 2025

Nirmaladevi Care Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Nirmaladevi Care Android mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information when using our Service, complying with applicable data protection laws including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect sensitive personal data automatically in the background. The app collects data only when voluntarily provided by you:

  • Customer Quotation Details: Name, phone number, company/factory address, and custom chemical order notes when requesting quotes.
  • Direct Communications: Contact data transmitted when you initiate phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or emails to our sales desk.
  • Local Calculation State: Chemical dilution and density calculation inputs are computed locally on your device and are never sent to external tracking servers.

2. Use of Information

Any information you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Process and generate wholesale chemical price quotations and delivery schedules.
  • Communicate regarding product grades (CP, Technical, Food Grade) and Certificates of Analysis (COA).
  • Provide customer support for industrial acid and alkali orders.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell, lease, or distribute your personal or commercial contact information to third parties or advertising networks. Data is communicated directly between you and Nirmaladevi Care Pvt. Ltd. via user-initiated standard intent apps (e.g. WhatsApp, Email client, Phone dialer).

4. Permissions Used by App

  • INTERNET: To connect to WhatsApp inquiry endpoints, load maps, and send inquiry emails.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is an industrial B2B chemical trading platform intended for business users and does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.
